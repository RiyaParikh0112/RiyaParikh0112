- 🤖 I spend most of my time thinking about agents — how they fail, how to evaluate them, and how to ship them to people who don't care how they work (they just want it to work)
- 🧠 I've been doing AI PM for a couple of years now. Before that I thought I wanted to be a data scientist. I was wrong, and I'm glad
- 🔬 A chunk of my work has been in **evals** — building frameworks to test, debug, and measure AI agents before they break in production (ideally before, anyway)
- 🛒 The rest lives in **e-commerce** — marketplaces, catalogs, inventory, the unglamorous infrastructure that makes products actually show up when you search for them
- ✍️ I write at **[AGI in Progress](https://agiinprogress.substack.com)** — working through ideas on AI, products, and what it means to build in this moment
- 📺 Sometimes I make videos. They live at **MoodyMind** and are deeply unrelated to AI
- 🎓 CS from IIIT Vadodara — which means I have strong opinions about data structures and even stronger opinions about tabs vs spaces (tabs, obviously)
